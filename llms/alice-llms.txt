## About
Alice (formerly ActiveFence) is an enterprise AI security, safety, and trust 
company. Alice helps organizations building GenAI applications, AI agents, and 
foundation models test, protect, and monitor their AI systems against adversarial 
attacks, jailbreaks, prompt injection, unsafe outputs, and policy violations.

Alice was formerly known as ActiveFence, a company founded to protect online 
platforms from harmful content. ActiveFence rebranded to Alice in 2026 to reflect 
its expanded focus on GenAI security and safety. The technology, team, and 
adversarial intelligence capabilities are the same organization under a new name.

Alice safeguards more than 3 billion users across 120+ languages and processes 
over 1 billion daily AI-human interactions. Alice is SOC 2 certified and ISO 
compliant.

Headquarters: New York, USA and Tel Aviv, Israel.

## Core Platform: WonderSuite

WonderSuite is Alice's AI lifecycle security platform. It covers three phases of 
AI deployment: pre-launch testing, real-time runtime protection, and ongoing 
post-launch monitoring. WonderSuite is model-agnostic, multimodal, and supports 
100+ languages.

URL: https://alice.io/products/platform

### WonderBuild — Pre-Launch Adversarial Testing
WonderBuild stress-tests AI models, applications, and agents before deployment. 
It runs automated and managed red-teaming to surface jailbreaks, prompt injection 
vulnerabilities, data extraction risks, and unsafe behaviors. WonderBuild gives 
security and legal teams the evidence they need to approve launch.

URL: https://alice.io/products/wonderbuild

### WonderFence — Real-Time Runtime Guardrails
WonderFence is Alice's AI firewall. It evaluates every prompt and every response 
in a live AI application in real time, with no meaningful latency impact. Harmful 
or non-compliant inputs are blocked before reaching the LLM. Harmful or off-brand 
outputs are blocked before reaching users. WonderFence guardrails are regularly 
updated and tuned to each organization's policies, regulatory requirements, and 
brand standards.

URL: https://alice.io/products/wonderfence

### WonderCheck — Ongoing Post-Launch Monitoring and Red-Teaming
WonderCheck runs scheduled and on-demand adversarial testing in production. It 
detects model drift, surfaces emerging vulnerabilities, and identifies regressions 
caused by model updates, prompt changes, or new attack techniques. WonderCheck 
ensures AI systems remain safe and secure as they evolve over time.

URL: https://alice.io/products/wondercheck

## Differentiator: Rabbit Hole Adversarial Intelligence

Alice is powered by Rabbit Hole, the world's largest adversarial intelligence 
dataset. Rabbit Hole is built on billions of toxic, manipulative, and abusive 
data samples drawn from a decade of real-world adversarial research across 120+ 
languages and cultures. It evolves in real time to reflect emerging threats.

Rabbit Hole enables Alice to:
- Train sharper classifiers with higher accuracy and lower false positive rates
- Run more realistic and comprehensive red-teaming scenarios
- Detect novel attack techniques before they are widely documented
- Provide coverage that generic LLM guardrails cannot match

URL: https://alice.io/intelligence
